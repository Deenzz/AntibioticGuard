Penicillin Allergy De-Labeling & Alternative Selector

Over 90% of labeled penicillin allergies are false or non-IgE mediated. Differentiate safely to avoid inappropriate reserve antibiotics.

Why Antibiotics Won't Help (English)

For Colds, Cough, Sore Throat, and Flu

YOUR INFECTION IS CAUSED BY A VIRUS:

IMPORTANT FACTS:

  • Antibiotics ONLY kill bacteria. They have zero effect on viruses (cold, flu, Covid-19, bronchitis).
  • Taking antibiotics unnecessarily causes side effects: diarrhea, stomach upset, severe fungal infections, and dangerous allergic reactions.
  • Overuse creates Superbugs (antibiotic-resistant bacteria) that may make future infections untreatable for you and your family.

EFFECTIVE SYMPTOM RELIEF:

  • Fever / Sore Throat: Paracetamol 500–1000 mg every 6 hours (max 4 g/day). Warm salt water gargles.
  • Runny Nose / Cough: Saline nasal spray, steam inhalation, warm honey and lemon drinks.
  • Rest & Hydration: Drink at least 2 liters of water daily and rest adequately.
Delayed Prescription (Preskripsi Tertangguh): If your symptoms do not improve after 3 days, or if you develop new high fever, contact the clinic to discuss if antibiotics are then required.
DANGER SIGNS - SEEK IMMEDIATE CARE: Difficulty breathing, swallowing saliva, chest pain, coughing blood, or blue lips.

Kenapa Antibiotik Tidak Diperlukan (BM)

Untuk Selsema, Batuk, Sakit Tekak, dan Demam Virus

JANGKITAN ANDA BERPUNCA DARI VIRUS:

FAKTA PENTING:

  • Antibiotik HANYA membunuh bakteria. Ia langsung tidak berkesan terhadap jangkitan virus seperti selsema dan batuk biasa.
  • Mengambil antibiotik tanpa keperluan mendatangkan kesan sampingan: cirit-birit, alahan kulit, dan gangguan bakteria baik usus.
  • Penggunaan berlebihan melahirkan Kuman Kebal (Bakteria Rintang) yang sukar diubati pada masa depan.

CARA MELEGAKAN SIMPTOM:

  • Demam / Sakit Tekak: Ambil Paracetamol jika perlu (tidak melebihi 4,000 mg sehari). Kumur air garam suam.
  • Hidung Tersumbat / Batuk: Semburan hidung saline, sedut wap air panas, dan minuman madu suam.
  • Rehat & Minum Air: Minum sekurang-kurangnya 2 liter air kosong sehari dan dapatkan rehat secukupnya.
Preskripsi Tertangguh: Sekiranya keadaan anda tidak bertambah baik selepas 3 hari, atau demam semakin tinggi, hubungi klinik semula untuk penilaian antibiotik.
TANDA BAHAYA - SEGERA KE KECEMASAN: Susah bernafas, tidak boleh menelan air liur, sakit dada, atau batuk berdarah.
